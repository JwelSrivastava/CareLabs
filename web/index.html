Book a test

This saves an appointment under your account. Pick a centre, one test that centre offers, and a time later than now. The amount is that centre’s price. Pay the appointment to confirm it, or cancel it while it is still pending.

  • The clock time you enter is your local time.
  • The same centre, test, and time can only be held once while the booking is pending or confirmed.
  • Payment does not charge a card. It returns paid or failed, and the appointment follows that result.
