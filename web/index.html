What booking a test does

Reserve the centre, the test, and the time before you walk in.

CareLabs holds one appointment under your name. The amount is the price that centre charges for that test, not a number you type. Paying the slip confirms the visit. Leaving it unpaid keeps it pending, and you can cancel until it is confirmed.

  1. 1Pick a centre that actually offers the test.
  2. 2Choose a future time on your own clock.
  3. 3Pay to confirm, or cancel while the slip is pending.

Before you book

The centre sets the price

Two labs can offer the same test at different amounts. The slip copies the price from the centre you pick.

One live booking per slot

You cannot keep two pending or confirmed bookings for the same centre, test, and time. Cancel the first one to free it.

Payment is a simulation

No card is charged. The desk marks the payment successful or failed, and the booking updates with it.

Your slips
